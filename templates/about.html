🏛 About Smart Civic Complaint Management System

🎯 Project Objective

The Smart Civic Complaint Management System is designed to provide an easy and transparent platform for citizens to register and track civic complaints online. The system helps municipal authorities improve public services and ensures faster complaint resolution.

✨ Features

  • ✅ Citizen Registration and Login
  • ✅ Online Complaint Registration
  • ✅ Complaint Tracking
  • ✅ Admin Dashboard
  • ✅ Complaint Resolution
  • ✅ Reports and Analytics
  • ✅ Citizen Feedback System
